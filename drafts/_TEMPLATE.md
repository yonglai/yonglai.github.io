## The claim

One or two sentences.

## Setup

What a reader needs to judge the evidence: for performance, hardware, versions, model and workload; for design or agent posts, the system, scale and constraints.

## Evidence

Pick what fits the pillar: benchmarks with variance (3+ runs) and a profile; traces or failure logs from real runs; eval results; or a design with its tradeoffs and a checked capacity or cost model.
Show the evidence first, then explain it.

## Why it happens

The mechanism. This is where the insight lives: not what changed, but why.

## What this does not show

Where this doesn't generalize: other hardware, models, scale, workloads, or constraints.

## Reproduce or verify it

Commands to rerun, or how a reader can check the claim themselves. Link the repo and commit in the frontmatter if there is code.
